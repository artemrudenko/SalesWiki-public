# Raw Sources

`raw/` stores source material before it is compiled into the wiki.

Rules:

- Keep original filenames where possible.
- Do not edit source files after ingest.
- Add a sidecar note when needed: `<source-name>.note.md`.
- Prefer dated folders for recurring imports.

Suggested structure:

- `raw/companies/` - company websites, about pages, product pages, press pages.
- `raw/news/` - collected news articles and search results.
- `raw/people/` - executive bios, interviews, social/public profiles.
- `raw/leads/` - lead lists, CRM exports, enrichment files.
- `raw/deals/` - opportunity exports, proposals, notes.
- `raw/calls/` - transcripts and call recordings metadata.
- `raw/campaigns/` - campaign briefs, performance exports, creative notes.
- `raw/crm/` - HubSpot exports, CRM snapshots, enrichment inputs.
- `raw/meetings/` - Google Meet references, meeting metadata, manual notes.
- `raw/events/` - exhibition/conference pages, agendas, sponsor lists, exhibitor lists, speaker lists.
- `raw/private-cases/` - dictated or written internal case inputs.
- `raw/assets/` - sales/marketing asset source files or references.
- `raw/kb/` - Google Drive KB references and exports.
- `raw/research/` - market research, audits, competitor scans.
- `raw/imports/` - unprocessed drops waiting for triage.
