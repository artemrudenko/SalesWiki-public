# Polish B2B starter

SalesWiki is most useful to small B2B teams that run on customer conversations,
repeat sales and follow-up, rather than high-volume one-off transactions.

Start with one account. Create a company card, record the business pain,
objection, commitment and dated next step from the latest conversation, then
open the daily queue. Do not migrate a whole CRM first.

The Workbench's `PL starter` panel is a guided demo of this path. It does not
store personal data, connect to a vendor, or claim that a Polish localisation is
complete. The durable templates and controls remain English today; Polish copy
is the next usability validation step with actual B2B users.

## Monitoring

The account monitoring dialog records the intended signal categories and review
cadence only in the current browser demo. It does not fetch news, call external
APIs, schedule a background job or notify anyone. A production monitor must use
the connector contracts, cite each collected source and enter durable knowledge
through the governed ingest/proposal path.
