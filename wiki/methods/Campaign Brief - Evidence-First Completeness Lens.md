# Campaign Brief - Evidence-First Completeness Lens

## Decision Supported

Choose the smallest testable campaign or content action that follows from a
visible audience signal and approved proof.

## Candidate Checklist

1. State the audience or segment only when a supplied signal supports it.
2. Connect the value proposition to approved proof; otherwise mark the proof
   gap instead of drafting a promise.
3. Name a channel, asset or experiment only as a testable proposal, not as a
   prediction of performance.
4. State the measurement outcome that would change the next marketing choice.
5. Keep current platform, legal and consent constraints outside the textbook
   and verify them from current primary documentation.

## Prompt Use

Use as a response-shape and evaluation checklist for `marketing ×
campaign_brief`. It preserves the Answer Contract and cannot introduce audience
facts, customer proof or performance claims.
