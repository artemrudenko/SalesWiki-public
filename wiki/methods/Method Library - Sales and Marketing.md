# Method Library - Sales and Marketing

This library turns licensed teaching and research sources into small,
decision-specific **method notes**. It is deliberately separate from entity
cards and from the Answer Contract.

## Boundary

A method note can improve a question checklist, response structure or synthetic
evaluation rubric. It cannot establish customer facts, infer access, hide
citations, change scoring weights, or be presented as an approved sales policy.

## Adoption Rule

Each note stays `candidate` until a reviewer compares the baseline and
method-informed prompt on the synthetic scenarios in
[[method-library-pilot]]. An adopted note must retain its source section,
limitations and test evidence.

See [[method-library-intake]] for the intake and license process.
