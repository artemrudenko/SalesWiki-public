# Sales Stage Assistant

The sales assistant should support the seller by stage.

## Stages

- research
- outreach
- qualification/MQL
- discovery
- intro call
- proposal
- negotiation
- closed won/lost
- account management

## Stage Outputs

## Research

- company brief
- person brief
- trigger summary
- lead score

## Outreach

- message angle
- personalization
- recommended channel
- reminder

## Qualification / MQL

- missing qualification data
- score update
- next best action
- nurture plan

## Discovery

- call prep
- discovery questions
- likely objections
- proof to use

## Intro Call

- call prep
- tailored intro narrative
- relevant case studies/assets
- future: client-specific intro deck

## Proposal

- deal readout
- stakeholder concerns
- competitor battlecard
- proof points

## Negotiation

- risk summary
- decision criteria
- blocker map
- next step

## Account Management

- account plan
- renewal/upsell triggers
- relationship history
- private cases
- delivery/project signals
