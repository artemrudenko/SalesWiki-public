# Report Templates

Different users need different reports. Do not force every report into one generic structure.

## HoS Weekly Report

Audience: Head of Sales.

Must include:

- pipeline risks
- stale deals
- promising leads without action
- MQL/qualification movement
- lead/deal score distribution
- overdue tasks
- source/channel signal quality
- recommended management actions

## Lead Monitoring Report

Audience: SDR/BDR, sales manager.

Must include:

- leads by segment
- new triggers
- score changes
- leads to touch now
- leads to wait on
- reminders created
- missing data

## Marketing Weekly Report

Audience: marketing manager, demand gen, content.

Must include:

- topic movement
- campaign performance
- content opportunities
- event/news hooks
- asset gaps
- sales feedback
- next experiments

## Campaign Performance Report

Audience: marketing and sales.

Must include:

- audience
- channel/source/medium
- offer/CTA
- MQL/SQL/opportunity impact
- asset performance
- sales handoff quality
- learnings
- next decision

## Data Quality Report

Audience: RevOps/Enablement.

Must include:

- duplicate entities
- stale pages
- missing required fields
- missing source links
- CRM/wiki conflicts
- index status
- access/redaction risks
