# Dashboard Contract

Dashboards are the employee-facing operational layer. They should answer "what needs attention now?" without requiring users to understand the vault structure.

Dashboards come in two forms:

- Obsidian Bases in `dashboards/` for interactive filtering inside Obsidian.
- Markdown snapshots in `reports/dashboard-snapshots/` generated by `scripts/build_dashboard_snapshots.py` for presentations, digests and non-technical review.

## Required Dashboards

| Dashboard | Audience | Primary question | Obsidian view | Snapshot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sales Today | SDR/BDR, sales manager | Who should we act on today? | `dashboards/sales-today.base` | `reports/dashboard-snapshots/sales-today.md` |
| Lead Priority | SDR/BDR, sales manager | Which leads are hot/warm or on the watchlist? | `dashboards/lead-priority.base` | included in Sales Today |
| Deal Risk | HoS, account owner, sales manager | Which deals need intervention? | `dashboards/deal-risk.base` | `reports/dashboard-snapshots/deal-risk.md` |
| Monitoring | sales/marketing/research owner | What is due or stale? | `dashboards/monitoring.base` | `reports/dashboard-snapshots/monitoring.md` |
| Marketing Insights | marketing manager, demand gen, content | What topics, pains, objections or campaign ideas should marketing use? | `dashboards/marketing-insights.base` | `reports/dashboard-snapshots/marketing-insights.md` |
| Data Quality / Review Queue | curator, RevOps, enablement | What is missing, risky or needs review? | `dashboards/review-queue.base`, `dashboards/data-quality.base` | `reports/dashboard-snapshots/data-quality.md` |

## Required Fields

Every dashboard row should expose enough context to decide the next action:

- entity/card name
- type
- owner or monitoring owner
- freshness/status
- confidence or score where relevant
- next review/check/action date where relevant
- access label when sensitivity affects sharing

## Snapshot Rules

Generated snapshots should:

- be readable without Obsidian plugins
- lead with counts and top action lists
- clearly say when there is no production data yet
- never include raw personal-data excerpts
- be generated from indexes/cards, not hand-written

## Update Rules

Run after real entity-card changes:

```bash
python3 scripts/build_indexes.py
python3 scripts/build_dashboard_snapshots.py
```

For demo or fixture vaults, pass explicit roots and do not update production state.
