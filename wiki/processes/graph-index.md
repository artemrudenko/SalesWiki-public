# Graph And Index Design

See also: `index-and-graph-maintenance.md` (operational when/how to rebuild) and `relationship-model.md` (required links per entity type). This page is the graph design and node/edge catalog.

The wiki remains the human-readable source of truth. Obsidian's built-in graph is the first graph layer. Baseline derived indexes are generated by `scripts/build_indexes.py`; richer search, clustering and graph analytics can build on top of those outputs later.

## Indexes

- Full-text index over `wiki/`.
- Entity index by normalized company/person names.
- Freshness index for stale pages.
- Temporal index for news and calls.
- Graph index for relationship traversal.
- Optional vector index for semantic recall over compiled pages.

Baseline generated files:

- `indexes/entities/entity-registry.csv`
- `indexes/entities/entities.jsonl`
- `indexes/fulltext/documents.jsonl`
- `indexes/freshness/freshness.jsonl`
- `indexes/graph/edges.jsonl`
- `indexes/temporal/events.jsonl`

`edges.jsonl` includes:

- `WIKILINKS_TO` edges from body wikilinks
- typed semantic edges from frontmatter fields, including company/deal/participant/source/duplicate/corroboration relationships
- exact duplicate edges are removed during generation

## Clustering Use Cases

- Similar companies by industry, size, product, pain and trigger.
- Leads by intent strength and likely buying committee.
- Deals by risk pattern.
- News by market movement, funding, hiring, leadership change, product launch.
- Events by topic, sponsor cluster, exhibitor overlap, target-account density and competitor presence.
- Calls by objection, pain, competitor, urgency.

## Health Checks

- Orphan pages without backlinks.
- Claims without sources.
- Duplicate companies or people.
- Stale active deals.
- Hot leads without next action.
- News items not linked to companies or people.
- Events without exhibitors, sponsors, speakers or source links.
