# Research And Audit Process

Use this process for company research, executive tracking, website checks, news monitoring and source audits.

## Company Research

Collect and compile:

- official website and product pages
- press/news page
- leadership pages
- careers page for hiring signals
- public funding, acquisition or expansion news
- competitor and category mentions
- recent articles from managed news resources
- open web search results

## Executive Tracking

For CEO, CTO, CMO, CRO, founders and key buyers:

- title changes
- interviews and public posts
- conference talks and podcasts
- product, hiring, funding or transformation signals
- leadership changes

## Audit Cadence

- Active deal companies: weekly.
- Hot leads: every 3 business days.
- Watchlist accounts: monthly.
- Source list quality: monthly.
- Full wiki hygiene audit: monthly.

## Output Format

Every research or audit run should answer:

- What changed?
- Which entities are affected?
- Why does it matter for marketing or sales?
- What should we do next?
- What sources support this?
- What did we already check before?
- What was duplicate or rejected?
- Which claims became stronger because multiple independent sources confirmed them?
