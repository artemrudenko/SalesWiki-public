# Sales And Marketing Research Framework

SalesWiki should be designed around user decisions, not around files. The system is useful only if it helps sales and marketing decide what to do next.

## Core Question

For every piece of information, ask:

- Who will use this?
- What decision does it support?
- When is it needed?
- What action should it change?
- What evidence supports it?
- How fresh does it need to be?

If the information does not support a decision, action, risk assessment or reusable learning, keep it in raw/tracking and do not over-promote it.

## Research Products

Research products are standardized outputs. They can be generated from many cards.

## Company Brief

Purpose: prepare for outreach, call or account planning.

Must include:

- snapshot
- strategic conclusions
- recent signals
- key people
- active leads/deals/calls
- relevant pain points
- relevant proof/assets
- next action
- source dates and confidence

## Person Brief

Purpose: understand a buyer, executive, champion or influencer.

Must include:

- role and company
- likely priorities
- recent articles/interviews/talks
- relationship status
- messaging angle
- risks or sensitivities
- next action
- evidence

## Lead Brief

Purpose: decide whether and how to contact.

Must include:

- lead type and priority
- why now
- trigger
- personalization
- suggested message
- owner and next action

## Call Prep

Purpose: help a sales person run a better meeting.

Must include:

- what matters now
- likely pains
- suggested questions
- objections to expect
- proof to use
- recent signals
- open gaps

## Call Readout

Purpose: turn a meeting into action and updated knowledge.

Must include:

- summary
- pains
- buying signals
- objections
- commitments
- deal risk
- updates to company/person/deal
- follow-up draft

## Deal Risk Report

Purpose: identify what could block revenue.

Must include:

- deal
- risk
- evidence
- missing next step
- owner
- recommended action

## Campaign Brief

Purpose: plan or refine marketing activity.

Must include:

- target ICP/persona
- pain point
- trigger
- message
- offer/CTA
- assets
- sales handoff
- success metric

## Event Brief

Purpose: use conferences/exhibitions for pipeline, content and intelligence.

Must include:

- dates/location
- topics
- exhibitors
- sponsors
- speakers
- target accounts
- competitors
- outreach opportunities
- source dates

## Weekly Signal Digest

Purpose: keep sales/marketing aligned on what changed.

Must include:

- top changes
- new target-account signals
- executive/person updates
- event updates
- corroborated claims
- duplicates/noise
- recommended actions
- gaps/failures

## Information Freshness By Use Case

| Use case | Freshness target |
| --- | --- |
| Call prep | same day or previous business day |
| Hot lead outreach | 1-3 business days |
| Active deal risk | weekly, or after every material interaction |
| Event week prospecting | daily during event week |
| Active account news | weekly |
| Executive tracking for active targets | 14 days |
| Campaign planning | before launch and at review checkpoints |
| ICP/persona/pain/objection knowledge | monthly or after meaningful call/deal evidence |
| Competitor intel in active deals | before competitive call or proposal |

## Research Promotion Rules

Raw material becomes a stronger wiki artifact only when it is useful:

- Raw source -> tracking entry: every reviewed item.
- Tracking entry -> evidence card: useful news/article/call/event participation.
- Evidence card -> claim: important factual assertion worth reusing.
- Claim/evidence -> entity conclusion: actionable insight for company/person/deal/event.
- Repeated insight -> reusable knowledge card: pain point, objection, use case, persona, competitor intel or case study.
- Reusable knowledge -> campaign/outreach/asset: when it should change execution.

## What Not To Over-Track

Avoid promoting:

- generic articles with no target relevance
- low-quality listicles
- copied press releases unless they confirm a primary announcement
- old news with no current action value
- minor executive mentions without relevance
- weak social posts unless they affect a target person/company/event

Keep these as rejected or low-value tracking entries instead.

## Minimum Answer Contract

Every employee-facing answer should include:

- answer first
- why it matters
- evidence and dates
- confidence
- recommended action
- what is missing or stale

## Employee Request Patterns

Employees should not need to know folders, schemas or indexes - they ask normal
business questions. These are the canonical request phrases and the product each
returns (full "must include" specs are in the Research Products section above, or
in the linked process doc).

| Employee asks (example) | Returns / see |
| --- | --- |
| `Дай свежий бриф по компании <name>` | Company Brief |
| `Подготовь меня к звонку с <company/person>` | Call Prep |
| `Покажи горячие лиды на этой неделе` | Lead Brief (hot-lead list) |
| `Какие сделки под риском?` | Deal Risk Report |
| `Что нам неизвестно по <company>?` | Research gap (missing facts, why they matter, quickest research path, owner/agent task) |
| `Дай бриф по выставке <event>` | Event Brief |
| `Кого из наших target accounts можно поймать на <event>?` | Event prospecting list (participation type, role, relationship, outreach action) - see [[event-roi-action-loop]] |
| `Оцени лида <lead/company/person>` | Lead Score (score/band, factors, evidence, confidence, next action, next review) - see [[scoring-models-v1]] |
| `Проверь MQL/qualification лиды` | MQL/qualification review - see [[lead-monitoring-and-scoring]] |
| `Кого из перспективных лидов вне пайплайна стоит тронуть?` | Outside-pipeline monitoring - see [[lead-monitoring-and-scoring]] |
| `Разбери Google Meet звонок <link>` | Call import + readout - see [[google-meet-call-import]] |
| `Обогати HubSpot карточку <company/person/deal>` | CRM enrichment proposal - see [[hubspot-enrichment]] |
| `Запиши приватный кейс по <project/client/challenge>` | Private case capture - see [[private-case-capture]] |
| `Сделай отчет для HoS за неделю` | HoS weekly report - see [[report-templates]] |
