## Event Page Types To Monitor

| Page type | What to collect | Cadence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage | dates, positioning, audience, main themes | weekly |
| Agenda | sessions, topics, speakers, timing | weekly, then daily during event week |
| Speakers | people, roles, companies, talks | weekly |
| Exhibitors | companies, booths, categories | weekly |
| Sponsors | companies, sponsorship levels | weekly |
| Partners | associations, media, ecosystem partners | biweekly |
| Awards/finalists | high-signal companies and categories | when published |
| Press/news | organizer announcements | weekly |

## Open Search Patterns

Use these patterns when event pages are incomplete or stale:

- `"<Event Name>" exhibitors`
- `"<Event Name>" sponsors`
- `"<Event Name>" speakers`
- `"<Event Name>" agenda`
- `"<Event Name>" "<Company Name>"`
- `"<Event Name>" "<Person Name>"`
- `"<Event Name>" site:linkedin.com`
- `"<Company Name>" "<Event Name>" booth`
- `"<Company Name>" "<Event Name>" speaker`
- `"<Topic>" conference "<Year>"`

## Collection Notes

- Event websites often update silently, so always record collection date.
- Sponsor and exhibitor pages can be paginated or dynamically loaded.
- Speaker pages may use headshots/cards without clean text; preserve source URL and extracted fields.
- Treat company blog posts and executive posts as secondary confirmation.
- Distinguish confirmed participation from inferred participation.
- Follow `wiki/processes/source-governance.md` for reliability, access and usage rules.
